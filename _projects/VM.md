* Research Objective: Investigated whether Bitcoin exhibits volatility dynamics similar to the S&P 500 and assessed whether stock market volatility can explain and forecast cryptocurrency volatility.
* Methodology: Conducted descriptive statistical analysis, performed ARCH tests to detect volatility clustering, and applied GARCH(1,1) and GJR-GARCH models to capture volatility persistence, asymmetric market responses, and cross-market volatility relationships.
* Key Findings: Found that Bitcoin is significantly more volatile than the S&P 500, exhibiting stronger persistence, greater tail risk, and higher sensitivity to market shocks. While S&P 500 volatility provides limited predictive power, Bitcoin volatility is primarily driven by asset-specific factors, demonstrating the limitations of traditional equity-based risk models for cryptocurrency markets.
