* Research Objective: Conducted a comprehensive investment analysis of China's gaming industry to identify the most attractive long-term investment opportunity among NetEase, 37 Interactive, and Perfect World.
* Methodology: Performed industry and company analysis by evaluating market trends, competitive positioning, financial performance, business models, and valuation using fundamental analysis and financial metrics to develop an evidence-based investment thesis.
* Key Findings: Identified NetEase as the strongest long-term investment opportunity due to its diversified game portfolio, global expansion strategy, stable financial performance, and attractive valuation, while highlighting key industry risks and future growth opportunities.
